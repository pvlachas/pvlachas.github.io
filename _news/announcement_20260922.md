I gave an online talk at the [Fifth International Nonlinear Dynamics Conference (NODYCON 2026)](https://nodycon.org), hosted by [**Sapienza University of Rome**](https://www.uniroma1.it/en), on **September 22, 2026**.

The talk, *Weight-Space Interpolation for Parametric Dynamical Systems via Hypernetworks with Learned Embeddings*, joint work with [Konstantinos Vlachas](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=C5TbAqsAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Eleni Chatzi](https://chatzi.ibk.ethz.ch), presented [PHLieNets](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2026.114635), published in [MSSP](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/mechanical-systems-and-signal-processing), and how a single hypernetwork model forecasts across an entire parametric family of **nonlinear dynamical systems**.
