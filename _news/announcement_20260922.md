I gave an online talk at the [Fifth International Nonlinear Dynamics Conference (NODYCON 2026)](https://nodycon.org), hosted by **Sapienza University of Rome**, on **September 22, 2026**.

The talk, *Weight-Space Interpolation for Parametric Dynamical Systems via Hypernetworks with Learned Embeddings*, joint work with Konstantinos Vlachas and Eleni Chatzi, presented [PHLieNets](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2026.114635) and how a single hypernetwork model forecasts across an entire parametric family of **nonlinear dynamical systems**.
