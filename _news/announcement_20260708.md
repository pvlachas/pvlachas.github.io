Our paper [*Beyond Static Models: Hypernetworks for Adaptive and Generalizable Forecasting in Complex Parametric Dynamical Systems*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymssp.2026.114635), with Konstantinos Vlachas and Eleni Chatzi, is now published open access in **Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing** (Volume 258, 2026).

The paper introduces [PHLieNets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19609), hypernetworks that learn an embedding of the system parameters and generate the weights of a forecasting network, so that a single model covers an entire family of **parametric dynamical systems** and generalizes to regimes unseen during training.
