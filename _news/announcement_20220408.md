The paper [Multiscale simulations of complex systems by learning their effective dynamics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-022-00464-w) was published in [Nature Machine Intelligence](https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/)! This is joint work with Georgios Arampatzis, [Caroline Uhler](https://carolineuhler.com/), and [Petros Koumoutsakos](https://seas.harvard.edu/person/petros-koumoutsakos). The code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/pvlachas/LearningEffectiveDynamics).
