Our paper [*Transcriptomics-Conditioned Virtual Tissue Synthesis via Diffusion Transformers*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.26.727902v1.abstract) has been accepted at the [**ICML 2026 Workshop on Structured Data for Health (SD4H)**](https://structureddata4health.github.io/) at [ICML 2026](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026).

The work, in collaboration with [Kalin Nonchev](https://bmi.inf.ethz.ch/people/person/kalin-nonchev), [Viktor Koelzer](https://dbe.unibas.ch/en/research/medical/computational-pathology/), and [Gunnar Rätsch](https://bmi.inf.ethz.ch/people/person/gunnar-raetsch), introduces a diffusion-transformer framework that conditions on transcriptomics to synthesize high-resolution virtual tissue, bridging **spatial omics** and **computational pathology**.
