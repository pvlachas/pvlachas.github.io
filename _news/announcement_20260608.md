Our paper [*Transcriptomics-Conditioned Virtual Tissue Synthesis via Diffusion Transformers*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.26.727902v1.abstract) has been accepted at the **ICML 2026 Workshop on Structured Data for Health (SD4H)**.

The work, in collaboration with Kalin Nonchev, Viktor Koelzer, and Gunnar Rätsch, introduces a diffusion-transformer framework that conditions on transcriptomics to synthesize high-resolution virtual tissue, bridging **spatial omics** and **computational pathology**.
